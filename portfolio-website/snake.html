Architecture direction

How the portfolio and game service split responsibilities

Portfolio frontend

Public marketing page, room UX, player identity, Spotify auth/session setup, and persistent history views.

Neon persistence

Stores room metadata, host linkage, track snapshots, match history, and leaderboard data. No real-time tick traffic.

Realtime game service

Owns the authoritative simulation loop, collision resolution, food spawning, and player movement state.
